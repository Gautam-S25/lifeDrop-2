How it works

From sign-up to superhero in four steps

The whole visit takes about an hour, and the donation itself only about ten minutes.

1

Register

Pick a camp and a time slot. Takes under two minutes and no paperwork.

2

Quick screening

A medical officer checks your pulse, blood pressure, weight and haemoglobin.

3

Donate

Relax on a comfortable bed while about 350–450 ml of blood is collected.

4

Rest & refresh

Enjoy snacks and a drink, collect your donor card, and tell your friends.

Know your type

Blood group compatibility checker

Pick your blood group to see who you can donate to — or who can donate to you. Matching blood groups is what makes transfusions safe.

  • O− is the universal donor — hospitals always need it.
  • AB+ is the universal recipient.
  • Every blood group is needed, every day.
